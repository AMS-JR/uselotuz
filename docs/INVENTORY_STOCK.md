# Inventory stock movements

Inventory tracks stock by business, item and location using an auditable movement ledger. V3 introduced opening balances and V4 introduced receipts. V5 adds signed adjustments with a required reason and an item-level, paginated movement history. V6 adds consumption movements. Earlier phase documents are historical records.

V7 adds optional per-item, per-location minimum thresholds. Owners and admins can set or remove a nonnegative threshold in the item's unit; staff can view it. A unit cannot be changed while a threshold or stock movements exist. Existing items have no threshold after migration.

V8 adds saved reorder plans. The Reorder List contains active items with an opening balance and configured minimum whose current balance is strictly below that minimum. Its suggested quantity is minimum minus current balance, calculated as an exact decimal. Items without an opening balance or minimum are excluded; an item exactly at its minimum remains a low-stock alert but is not on the reorder list. Owners and admins may save or clear a planned quantity; staff may view and export plans. Plans do not change stock, are retained if an item later leaves the list, and reappear if it falls below its minimum again. Check saved plans against current suggestions before acting.

`GET /api/businesses/{businessId}/inventory/reorder` accepts `locationId`, `q`, `type`, `supplierId`, `page` and `size`. `GET /reorder/export` exports all matching rows with the same filters. `PUT /reorder/{itemId}/plan` accepts `locationId` and a decimal-string `quantity`, or null to clear the plan. Existing business, Inventory entitlement, role and CSRF checks apply. CSV text fields use the catalog export's spreadsheet-safe escaping; validated quantities are exported as plain decimal numbers.

Stock is not established until an opening movement exists. Established zero is out of stock, even without a threshold. Positive stock at or below a configured minimum is low; above it is in stock. Positive stock with no minimum is shown as having no threshold rather than being classified as low. Archived items are excluded from Overview alert counts. Items can be filtered for low or out-of-stock balances before pagination. Alerts are computed from the ledger at request time; there are no background or email alerts.

An opening balance must be established before a receipt, adjustment or consumption; zero is a valid opening balance. Current stock is the sum of movements. Owners and admins may record receipts, adjustments and consumption; staff may read stock and history. Archived items remain readable but cannot receive new stock movements. Membership, Inventory entitlement and product role are checked on each request; mutations require the existing session CSRF protection.

Quantities are decimal strings in the API and use BigDecimal and NUMERIC(20,6). PIECE quantities must be whole. Adjustments are signed, nonzero changes in the item's existing unit and may not reduce stock below zero. Units cannot be changed after stock has been established. Receipts, adjustments and consumption lock the business and item in a transaction and require business-scoped idempotency keys; a replay with different data is rejected. Opening balances lock the item.

All routes use `/api/businesses/{businessId}/inventory/stock`. Existing opening and receipt routes remain available. `POST /{itemId}/adjustments` accepts `locationId`, signed `quantity`, `reason`, and `idempotencyKey`; it returns the movement ID and current quantity. `POST /{itemId}/consumption` accepts the same fields with a positive quantity and records a negative movement; insufficient stock is rejected. `GET /{itemId}/movements?locationId=...&page=0&size=25` returns a newest-first page of movements with the actor ID, timestamp, unit, quantity and applicable reason or receipt metadata. Page sizes are limited to 100.

`PUT /{itemId}/threshold` accepts a location ID and a decimal string `quantity`; use null to remove it. `GET /alerts?locationId=...` returns low and out-of-stock counts for active items. The item list accepts `stockStatus=low` or `out` with `locationId`.

On Items, item names open Product Details with stock, supplier, paginated movement history and the available item and stock actions. Opening, receiving, adjustments, consumption and minimum settings open dialogs; the Actions menu also provides quick access without expanding the table row. Successful stock changes update the balance and reload the movement history in Product Details. All Inventory roles can open item details and history, including for archived items; management actions remain limited to owners and admins for active items. The signed-in actor is shown by name; other actors are identified by their stored user ID because the stock API does not return a user directory. Overview alert cards link to the corresponding Items filter. Reorder planning opens a dialog and retains the same filtered CSV export. The existing catalog workflows are unchanged. Physical counts and valuation remain future work.

Run backend tests only against a disposable database: the integration fixtures delete their test data. `scripts/verify-postgres.sh` starts a separate PostgreSQL 18 container and runs the backend verification suite; it does not use the development volume. Frontend verification uses `pnpm lint`, `pnpm typecheck`, the Inventory build and Playwright catalog tests. Browser fixtures do not replace manual authentication and database acceptance.

## Basic reports

Reports provides a current-stock view for active items and a read-only, newest-first movement view including historical movements for archived items. The location-scoped status calculation preserves the existing `NO_THRESHOLD` distinction. The movement period defaults to the last 30 UTC calendar days; both selected dates are inclusive. Summary cards show active items, low and out-of-stock counts, and all movements in the selected period. Quantities remain in each item's own unit.

Read-only endpoints under `/api/businesses/{businessId}/inventory/reports` are `GET /stock`, `/movements`, `/summary`, `/stock/export` and `/movements/export`. All require `locationId` and active Inventory access; owners, admins and staff may view and export. Stock filters are `q`, `type`, `categoryId`, and `status` (`IN_STOCK`, `LOW_STOCK`, `OUT_OF_STOCK`, `NOT_ESTABLISHED`, or `NO_THRESHOLD`). Movement filters are `q`, `movementType`, and `from`/`to` ISO dates. Paginated views accept `page` and `size` (1–100). CSV exports apply their report filters, escape text using catalog spreadsheet-safety rules, and output validated quantities as numeric values. No migration was added. Run `./mvnw test` in `backend` against the default disposable H2 database or `bash scripts/verify-postgres.sh` for the isolated PostgreSQL suite; frontend checks include the Inventory Playwright reports tests.

## Physical counts and reconciliation

Stock Counts lets owners and admins create a draft for 1–100 distinct active items at a location. Each item must already have opening stock. Creation captures the ledger balance and movement count; users can save nonnegative physical quantities (whole numbers for PIECE) and resume a draft or in-progress session. Saved variance is physical minus recorded quantity. Staff have read-only access to count sessions. Counts and completed history remain business- and location-scoped.

Confirming a fully saved count locks the business and selected items, checks that no movements have been added since each snapshot, and writes any nonzero variances through the existing adjustment service in the same transaction. An offsetting pair of movements is still a conflict. If stock changed, confirmation fails without changing stock; **Refresh and recount** replaces the snapshots and clears all entered quantities, requiring a new physical count. Completion is recorded with the actor and timestamp, and closed counts cannot be edited or confirmed again. A session version rejects stale saves, cancellations, refreshes and confirmations. Cancellation closes a count without changing inventory. Adjustment history includes the count ID in its reason.

`GET /api/businesses/{businessId}/inventory/counts?locationId=...&page=0&size=25` lists sessions; `GET /counts/{id}` returns details. Owners/admins use `POST /counts` with `locationId` and `itemIds`, `PUT /counts/{id}` with `version` and the complete item/quantity list, and `POST /counts/{id}/confirm`, `/refresh`, or `/cancel` with `version`. Existing authentication, business and Inventory grants, and CSRF checks apply. V9 adds tenant-scoped count-session and item tables; it does not alter existing stock movements or balances.

Inventory access is separate from business membership. Business Owners can explicitly
grant or revoke Inventory access for employees in Workspace Membership; Inventory Admins
can manage stock and Staff can view it. See `AUTH0_WORKSPACE.md` for invitations and
membership lifecycle details.
